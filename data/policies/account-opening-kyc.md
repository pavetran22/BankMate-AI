# Nusa Bank Account Opening and KYC Requirements

> Nusa Bank is a fictional bank. These documents are synthetic and exist only to demo the BankMate AI assistant.

## Savings Account

The Nusa Savings Account can be opened with a minimum initial deposit of RM20. There is no minimum balance requirement and no monthly fee. Interest is calculated daily and credited monthly at 0.25% per annum on balances up to RM10,000 and 0.50% per annum on the portion above RM10,000.

## Current Account

The Nusa Current Account requires a minimum initial deposit of RM500 and comes with a cheque book on request. A monthly service fee of RM10 applies if the average monthly balance falls below RM1,000.

## eKYC Online Account Opening

Malaysian citizens aged 18 and above can open a Savings Account fully online through the Nusa mobile app using electronic Know Your Customer (eKYC). The process requires a photo of the front and back of the MyKad and a live selfie video for face matching. Accounts opened through eKYC have a daily transfer limit of RM3,000 for the first 30 days.

## Documents Required at a Branch

Malaysian citizens need their original MyKad. Non-Malaysians need their passport, a valid work or student pass with at least 6 months' validity, and a letter from their employer or institution.

Minors aged 12 to 17 can open a joint savings account with a parent or legal guardian, who must bring their own MyKad and the minor's MyKid or birth certificate.

## Customer Due Diligence

As required by anti-money laundering regulations, Nusa Bank must verify each customer's identity, understand the purpose of the account, and understand the expected source of funds. Customers may be asked to provide additional documents, such as proof of income or source of wealth, when opening an account or later during periodic reviews.

Nusa Bank may decline to open an account, or may close an existing account, if a customer cannot provide the information required for due diligence.

## Dormant Accounts

An account with no customer-initiated transactions for 12 months is classified as dormant. Dormant accounts cannot be used for online transfers until reactivated at a branch. After 7 years of dormancy, the balance is transferred to the Registrar of Unclaimed Moneys as required by law.
