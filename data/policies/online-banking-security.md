# Nusa Bank Online Banking, Transfer Limits and Security

> Nusa Bank is a fictional bank. These documents are synthetic and exist only to demo the BankMate AI assistant.

## Daily Transfer Limits

The default daily limit for online transfers to third-party accounts is RM10,000, combined across DuitNow and Interbank GIRO (IBG). Customers can change their daily limit from RM0 to RM50,000 in the Nusa mobile app. Any increase takes effect after a 12-hour cooling-off period, while a decrease takes effect immediately.

Transfers between a customer's own Nusa Bank accounts are not subject to the daily limit.

## Transfer Fees

DuitNow transfers of up to RM5,000 are free. DuitNow transfers above RM5,000 cost RM0.50 per transaction. Interbank GIRO (IBG) transfers cost RM0.10 per transaction.

## Secure Device and Transaction Approval

Each customer can register one mobile device as their Secure Device. High-risk actions must be approved on the Secure Device using the in-app approval feature instead of an SMS OTP. High-risk actions include adding a new payee, increasing the transfer limit, and making transfers above RM5,000.

Registering a new Secure Device triggers a 12-hour cooling-off period during which high-risk actions are blocked.

## Kill Switch

If a customer suspects their account has been compromised, they can activate the Kill Switch in the Nusa mobile app or by calling the fraud hotline at 1-300-88-6872. The Kill Switch immediately freezes online banking, debit cards and credit cards. To reactivate access, the customer must visit a branch with their MyKad.

## Login Security

Online banking accounts are locked after 3 consecutive failed login attempts. Customers can unlock their account through the "Forgot Password" flow, which requires approval on the Secure Device, or by visiting a branch.

Sessions automatically log out after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## Safe Banking Practices

Customers should never share their password, PIN or OTP with anyone, including people claiming to be from Nusa Bank, the police or a government agency. Nusa Bank will never send links in SMS messages asking customers to log in. Customers should only download the Nusa mobile app from the official Apple App Store, Google Play Store or Huawei AppGallery.
