# Nusa Bank Credit Card Terms and Fees

> Nusa Bank is a fictional bank. These documents are synthetic and exist only to demo the BankMate AI assistant.

## Annual Fee

The Nusa Platinum card has an annual fee of RM250 for the principal card and RM125 for each supplementary card. The annual fee is waived for the first year. From the second year onwards, the fee is waived automatically if the cardholder makes at least 12 retail transactions of any amount within the card anniversary year.

The Nusa Classic card has no annual fee for life.

## Interest and Finance Charges

Retail purchases are interest-free if the full statement balance is paid by the payment due date. The interest-free period is up to 50 days from the transaction date.

If only part of the balance is paid, finance charges apply to the outstanding balance from the date each transaction was posted. The finance charge rate is tiered based on payment history over the past 12 months:

- Tier 1: 15% per annum for cardholders who paid the minimum payment on time for 12 consecutive months.
- Tier 2: 17% per annum for cardholders who paid the minimum payment on time for 10 out of 12 months.
- Tier 3: 18% per annum for all other cardholders.

## Minimum Payment

The minimum monthly payment is 5% of the total outstanding balance or RM50, whichever is higher. If the outstanding balance is below RM50, the full balance must be paid.

## Late Payment Charge

If the minimum payment is not received by the payment due date, a late payment charge of 1% of the total outstanding balance is applied, subject to a minimum of RM10 and a maximum of RM100.

## Cash Advance

Cash withdrawals using the credit card are allowed at ATMs displaying the Visa or Mastercard logo. A cash advance fee of 5% of the amount withdrawn, with a minimum of RM25, is charged for each withdrawal. Interest at 18% per annum is charged on cash advances from the date of withdrawal until full repayment. There is no interest-free period for cash advances.

## Overseas Transactions

Transactions made in a foreign currency are converted to Ringgit Malaysia at the card network exchange rate on the posting date, plus a foreign currency conversion fee of 1.25% of the converted amount.

Online purchases from merchants registered outside Malaysia may also attract the 1.25% fee even when they are charged in Ringgit Malaysia.

## Credit Limit

The credit limit is assigned based on income and credit assessment. Cardholders may request a temporary credit limit increase of up to 30% for a period of up to 3 months by calling customer service or through the Nusa mobile app. Permanent limit increases require updated income documents.

Transactions that exceed the credit limit will be declined. Nusa Bank does not charge an over-limit fee.
