# Nusa Bank Personal Loan FAQ

> Nusa Bank is a fictional bank. These documents are synthetic and exist only to demo the BankMate AI assistant.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be Malaysian citizens or permanent residents aged 21 to 60 at the time of application, with a minimum gross monthly income of RM3,000. Self-employed applicants must have been in business for at least 2 years.

## Loan Amount and Tenure

Personal loans are available from RM5,000 to RM150,000, up to a maximum of 8 times the applicant's gross monthly income. The repayment tenure ranges from 2 to 7 years.

## Interest Rates

Personal loans use a flat interest rate from 6.5% to 11.0% per annum, depending on the applicant's credit profile, income and tenure. The effective interest rate is higher than the flat rate because interest is calculated on the original loan amount throughout the tenure. The effective rate is shown in the Product Disclosure Sheet before the customer accepts the offer.

## Documents Required

Salaried applicants need a copy of their MyKad, the latest 3 months' salary slips, and the latest 3 months' bank statements showing salary credits. Self-employed applicants need a copy of their MyKad, business registration documents, the latest 6 months' business bank statements, and their latest income tax return (Form B).

## Approval and Disbursement

A decision is usually given within 2 working days after all documents are received. Approved loans are disbursed to a Nusa Bank savings or current account within 1 working day of the customer accepting the offer.

## Early Settlement

Customers may fully settle the loan early at any time. An early settlement fee of 1% of the outstanding principal applies if the loan is settled within the first 3 years. No fee applies after 3 years. Unearned interest is rebated using the method set out in the loan agreement.

## Late Payment

A late payment charge of 1% per annum is applied on the overdue instalment amount, calculated daily until the overdue amount is paid. Persistent late payments are reported to the Central Credit Reference Information System (CCRIS) and may affect future credit applications.

## Stamp Duty

Stamp duty of 0.5% of the loan amount is payable on the loan agreement and is deducted from the loan amount at disbursement.
