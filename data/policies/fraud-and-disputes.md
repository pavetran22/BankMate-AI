# Nusa Bank Fraud Protection and Dispute Policy

> Nusa Bank is a fictional bank. These documents are synthetic and exist only to demo the BankMate AI assistant.

## Reporting Unauthorised Transactions

Customers must report any unauthorised or suspicious transaction as soon as possible, and no later than 60 days from the statement date on which the transaction first appeared. Reports can be made through the 24-hour fraud hotline at 1-300-88-6872, through the "Report Fraud" option in the Nusa mobile app, or at any branch.

Once a report is received, the affected card is blocked immediately to prevent further losses and a replacement card is issued within 7 working days at no charge.

## Customer Liability

A customer is not liable for unauthorised transactions made after the loss or theft has been reported to the bank.

For unauthorised transactions made before the report, the customer's liability is limited to RM250, provided the customer:

- did not act fraudulently,
- did not delay reporting without a reasonable cause, and
- did not share their PIN, password or one-time password (OTP) with any other person.

If the customer shared their OTP or PIN, or acted with gross negligence, the customer may be fully liable for the transactions.

## Dispute Investigation Timeline

After a dispute is lodged, Nusa Bank will acknowledge it within 2 working days and complete its investigation within 14 working days for domestic transactions and 45 working days for international transactions or transactions requiring a card network chargeback.

During the investigation, the disputed amount is placed on hold and no finance charges or late payment charges will be applied to it.

## Temporary Credit

For disputed card transactions above RM500, a temporary credit for the disputed amount is posted to the account within 10 working days while the investigation continues. If the investigation finds the transaction was valid, the temporary credit is reversed and the customer is notified in writing with the reasons.

## Fraud Monitoring

Nusa Bank monitors card and account activity in real time. Transactions may be held or declined, and the customer contacted by SMS or phone, when activity looks unusual. Common triggers include:

- a transaction much larger than the customer's normal spending,
- a transaction in a country the customer has not transacted in before,
- several small transactions in quick succession, which can indicate card testing,
- transactions between midnight and 5am, and
- purchases at high-risk merchant categories such as gift cards, cryptocurrency exchanges and online gambling.

Nusa Bank will never ask for a full PIN, password or OTP by phone, SMS or email.

## Scam Awareness

If a customer believes they were tricked into transferring money to a scammer, they should call the fraud hotline immediately and also contact the National Scam Response Centre at 997. Funds sent through authorised transfers can only be recovered if they have not yet been withdrawn by the recipient, so speed matters.
